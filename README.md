# Academic Collaborations

A public portfolio of academic–industry collaboration in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, engineering simulation, postgraduate supervision, and research translation.

This repository documents selected collaborations, research projects, conference outputs, publications, and supervision activities. It is organised by institution so that each university collaboration can grow independently while remaining part of one coherent portfolio.

## Collaboration areas

- AI and machine learning methodology
- Computer vision and image processing
- Physics-informed and engineering AI
- Computational modelling and simulation
- Time-series forecasting and smart energy systems
- Postgraduate supervision and technical mentoring
- Research evaluation and experimental design
- Industry–academia knowledge exchange
- Research-to-industry translation

## Featured projects

### Lithium-ion battery cathode porous-media prediction

An interdisciplinary computational framework combining computational fluid dynamics, design of experiments, MATLAB-based porous-media generation, image processing, lattice-Boltzmann modelling, and convolutional neural networks.

[View the University of Leeds battery cathode case study](./collaborations/university-of-leeds/projects/battery-cathode-porous-media/README.md)

### Short-term prosumer electricity-demand forecasting

An anonymised MSc Data Science case study covering data cleansing, shape-factor engineering, feature selection, k-means behavioural clustering, regression benchmarking, dynamic retraining, aggregation analysis, and baseline-aware evaluation for smart-grid flexibility.

[View the Aston University prosumer forecasting case study](./collaborations/aston-university/projects/prosumer-demand-forecasting/README.md)

## Public-content principles

This repository contains only material suitable for public sharing. Project pages distinguish personal contribution, collaborative contribution, student contribution, and institutional contribution. Confidential data, unpublished proprietary material, personal student information, client material, assessment content, and restricted research outputs are excluded.

## Attribution

Collaborative work should retain appropriate attribution to contributors and institutions. Repository summaries are portfolio descriptions and do not replace the official publication record.
