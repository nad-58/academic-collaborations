# University of Leeds — Supervision and Advisory Activities

## Industrial Advisory Board contribution

I contribute to the Engineering Programme as an Industrial Advisory Board member, providing industry-informed feedback on AI, machine learning, computer vision, digital twins, and emerging engineering technologies.

My contribution includes:

- advising on the integration of AI and data-driven methods into engineering education;
- supporting alignment between academic teaching and industrial practice;
- providing feedback on technical skills, employability, and future engineering needs;
- encouraging responsible and trustworthy use of AI in engineering applications.

## Postgraduate supervision

I have contributed as an industry adviser and co-supervisor to postgraduate projects involving applied AI, image processing, computational modelling, and engineering simulation.

Typical supervision activities include:

- refining project scope and research questions;
- advising on dataset preparation and model design;
- reviewing evaluation methodology and experimental evidence;
- supporting interpretation of results;
- helping students connect academic methods with practical engineering use cases;
- contributing to publication and dissemination planning.

## Public-content boundary

Only public-safe summaries are included in this repository. Student personal information, unpublished assessment material, confidential datasets, and restricted project details are excluded.
